# Day 06 - Business Card

A professional digital business card with social media links and hover effects.

## What I learned / practiced:

- Using **Font Awesome** icons (`fab fa-github`, `fab fa-linkedin`, `fab fa-twitter`)
- **Flexbox** layout: `display: flex`, `flex-direction: column`, `align-items: center`
- **Gap** property for spacing between items
- **Hover effects** with `:hover` and `transition`
- **Styling links** as buttons with custom colors
- **Box-shadow** and **border-radius** for modern design
